  PROMPT 1 — ONCE


Build Your Brand Voice File                   🧬
   I've attached my brand-voice template (brand-voice-template.md). Interview me to
   fill it in — ask ONE question at a time, and push back when my answers are vague
   or generic.

   Cover: who I am in one sentence, exactly who my audience is and what they want,
   my offers and calls-to-action, my voice (funny↔serious, bold↔humble,
   casual↔polished), 5 phrases I actually say, what I'd NEVER post, 3 short TRUE
   stories from my work with real details and numbers, and my 3-5 forever topics.

   Then save the completed file as brand-voice.md, keeping the per-platform style
   sections from the template. Write it so an AI reading ONLY this file could post
   as me — and my followers wouldn't notice the difference.



This file is the whole secret. Everyone else's AI posts sound like AI. Yours will sound like you — because
it's built from your stories, your phrases, your rules.
  PROMPT 2 — THE MASTER PROMPT (daily)


One Prompt, Any Platform               🎯
   Read my brand-voice.md. Then interview me — exactly 3 questions, ONE at a time.
   Wait for my answer before the next question:

   1. Which platform am I posting to today — Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok,
   Facebook, or YouTube?
   2. What's the topic — something that happened to me, something I want to teach,
   an offer to push… or should you research what's trending in my niche right now
   and pitch me 5 angles?
   3. What's the goal — grow followers, get DMs and leads, sell an offer, or start
   conversations?

   Then, with no further questions: research online what's working on that platform
   for that kind of content THIS week (hooks, formats, length). Write me 3 post
   options that follow the platform rules in my brand-voice file, in MY voice —
   every option must include at least one specific story, number, or phrase from my
   file that nobody else could post. No AI-speak ("game-changer", "unlock", "delve",
   rhetorical-question openers). End each with ONE call-to-action from my file that
   matches my goal.

   I'll pick one. If a visuals tool (like Higgsfield) is connected, generate a
   matching visual in my visual style — correct format for the platform (vertical
   9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 4:5 for feed posts). If my posting tool (like Blotato) is
   connected, show me the final post + proposed time and schedule it ONLY after I
   say yes. Never post without my approval. If nothing is connected, give me the
   final post ready to copy-paste.



That's the whole system. Three answers from you → researched, on-brand, platform-native post →
approved → published.

   The Full Week on Autopilot                   ♻️
      Read my brand-voice.md. Ask me the same 3 questions, but for the WEEK: which
      platform(s), any topics or news from my week I should work in (or research
      trending angles), and my #1 goal this week.

      Then: plan 5-7 posts across the week (mix formats the platform rewards — for
      Instagram: reels, carousels, singles; for X: posts + one thread; for LinkedIn:
      stories + one contrarian take). Show me the plan as a table and WAIT for my
      edits. After I approve: write every post per my brand-voice rules, generate the
      visuals if my visuals tool is connected, then show me the complete queue — day,
      time, platform, text, visual — and schedule it through my posting tool only after
      my explicit yes.

      Next week, start by telling me which of last week's posts did best and apply what
      we learned.



   Your new routine: answer 3 questions, approve a plan, approve a queue. ~15 minutes a week.

The rules that make this work



        The brand-voice file does the heavy lifting. One new story added to it = weeks of fresh,
        unfakeable content.

        Approval is a feature, not friction. Claude drafts and schedules; nothing goes live
        without your yes.

        Platform rules live in the file — hooks ≤8 words, IG max 5 hashtags, X ≤280, LinkedIn
        150-300 words with white space. Claude reads them every time, so every post lands
      native.

      If a post could belong to a competitor, delete it. The "would my followers believe I
      wrote this?" test beats every growth hack.
