How to use it: copy the template below into a file called brand-voice.md (the ready-to-fill .md file comes
with this pack). Upload it to Claude — or keep it in your Claude Code folder — and say: "Interview me to fill
in my brand voice file." Claude asks you a few questions, fills it in, and from then on every post it writes is
in your voice, with your stories, on your rules.


   ⚠️ The example answers below belong to "Alex," a fictional Austin coffee roaster — so you can see what
   GOOD looks like. Specific beats impressive: real numbers, real phrases, real stories. Replace everything with
   yours.



    WHO I AM
    Alex — I roast coffee in small batches in Austin and ship it nationwide. 8 years, one
    roastery, zero shortcuts.

    One sentence. What you do + the detail that makes you different.




    AUDIENCE
    Home-coffee nerds aged 25-45 who own a grinder, hate bitter coffee, and want café results
    without café prices.

    Who follows you (or should)? What do they WANT that they don't have yet?




    OFFERS & CALLS-TO-ACTION (rotate — one per post, never m ore )
    1. Free shipping on first order → "link in bio"                    2. Coffee quiz → "DM me QUIZ"   3.
    Wholesale → "DM me WHOLESALE"             4. Engagement → "tell me in the comments"




    VOICE RULES
    Funny↔Serious: 70% playful, serious about craft · Bold↔Humble: bold about coffee, humble
    about me · Casual↔Polished: casual, like a text to a friend · Short↔Storyteller: short
    lines, one story post a week
    NEVER: hashtag walls, "elevate your coffee experience", fake urgency, trashing competitors,
    politics
  MY PHRASES (how I actually talk)
  "life's too short for bitter coffee" · "roasted Tuesday, at your door Friday" · "your
  grinder matters more than your machine" · "okay real talk"




  MY STORIES (true + specific = the anti-AI-slo p fu el )
  WIN: a Dallas café switched to us — espresso sales up 40% in two months; the owner texted
  me a photo of the empty hopper.
  FAILURE: burned a 60lb batch in 2021 trusting a new thermometer over my nose — $800 of
  beans became garden mulch.
  SURPRISE: my best-seller started as a mistake — pulled a batch 90 seconds early and
  customers begged for "that lighter one."




  PROOF I CAN CLAIM
  8 years roasting · 40+ wholesale cafés · 4.9★ from 2,100 reviews · roast-to-door in 4 days




  CONTENT PILLARS (my forever topics)
  1. Brew better at home          2. Behind the roaster          3. Coffee myths, busted             4. Customer wins
    5. New roast drops




Platform styles — Claude reads the section for whichever platform you name


  📸 INSTAGRAM
    Hook = first line, max 8 words (assume it's cut off after that)
    Short lines, white space, 1-2 emoji max · MAX 5 hashtags, niche, at the end
    Formats: Reels (30s, hook in first 2 sec), carousels (slide 1 = big short text), single photos
    Visual style: warm tones, real hands + real coffee, no stocky perfection




  🐦 X / TWITTER
    Every post ≤280 characters. Punchy. Hot takes welcome. No hashtags (or one, max)
    Threads: first tweet must work alone; number the rest; end with the CTA




  💼 LINKEDIN
    150-300 words, one-line paragraphs, white space · max 3 hashtags
    Angle: business lessons from the work (not just tips) · still sounds like me
    No links in the post body — "link in comments"
🎵 TIKTOK
  30-second scripts: spoken words + on-screen text
  Hook in the FIRST 2 seconds, visual and specific ("I burned $800 of coffee — smell this")
  Casual, imperfect, behind-the-scenes energy




👥 FACEBOOK
  Conversational, 40-80 words, like talking to regulars · end with a question
  Local angle whenever possible



▶️ YOUTUBE COMMUNITY / SHORTS
  Community: one image + one question or poll · Shorts: TikTok rules, slightly less slang
